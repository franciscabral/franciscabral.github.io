What I build

Four layers, bottom to top.

The agent work sits on top of the integration work. That order matters — it's why the agents have anything trustworthy to act on.

  • 01

    Agent control planes

    Mandate-driven development control planes, plus the governance, skills and operating tooling that decide what an agent is allowed to do, what it must prove, and what it can never touch. Written in TypeScript and Go.

  • 02

    Document AI & intelligent capture

    Extraction, validation and hand-off pipelines for invoices and financial documents — portal configuration, custom extensions, and the tooling around them, wired straight into downstream ERP and procurement systems.

  • 03

    Finance & procurement integration

    A dozen-plus production integrations between procurement and ERP platforms: payments, tax determination, cost-centre and master-data sync, purchase-order flows and AP automation — across retail, fintech, logistics and services.

  • 04

    Operational tooling

    The unglamorous things that make the above usable: inventory cycle-count web and native mobile apps, credential and client vaults, schema-inference utilities, and internal developer tooling.
