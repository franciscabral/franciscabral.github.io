The arcade.

Four p5.js sketches written while learning the library. They're archived rather than deleted — untouched source, original canvas sizes, still playable.

The previews below are dependency-free canvas re-cuts. Click through for the real p5 versions.

Star Travel — Viagem nas Estrelas

A thousand stars projected through a shrinking z-axis. The oldest trick in creative coding and still the best one.

Life Game — Jogo da Vida

Conway's cellular automaton on a wrapping grid, seeded from the classic patterns. Click to reseed.

Rhythm Style

Four lanes of arrows falling toward a judgement line. Play it with the arrow keys.

Snake — Cobrinha

The classic, on a 20-pixel grid at ten frames a second. Arrow keys to steer.
